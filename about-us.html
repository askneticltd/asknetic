Who We Are

Built to secure opportunity across Tanzania.

Asknetic Enterprises Ltd was established in 2023 as a group of companies in Tanzania, located in the commercial capital, Dar es Salaam, with the intention of securing lucrative business and investment opportunities within Tanzania.

We strive to provide products, services and brands that go well with our customers' choices. Our range of services spans medical services, car hire, agro crop, ICT and digital solutions, building maintenance, real estate, general supplies, and air travel agency and visa assistance, along with products and brands that are safe for the environment and for consumers' health.

We pride ourselves on employing the best and brightest individuals, using the highest quality materials and the latest construction methods, so our customers receive the level of quality and value they deserve.

Our Journey

The Asknetic Story

From our founding in 2023 to today, follow our journey of growth, expansion, and operational excellence across Tanzania.

2023

Company Founded

Asknetic Enterprises Ltd was established in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania with a vision to provide premium, dependable business solutions.

2023

Service Expansion

Launched multiple service divisions including ICT Solutions, Building Maintenance, and Agro Crop export operations.

2024

Growing Clientele

2025

Zanzibar Operations

Extended our car hire and travel agency services to cover Zanzibar, seamlessly serving both mainland and island clients.

2026
