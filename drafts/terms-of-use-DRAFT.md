# DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW — NOT APPROVED, DO NOT PUBLISH

**Status:** prepared on 14 October 2026 by the website developer. This is a checklist of topics, **not** terms
of use: it contains no legal wording, and none should be invented by a developer. The owner and a legal adviser
decide whether the website needs terms of use at all and, if so, write them. This folder is not part of the
upload package and nothing here is linked from the website.

When an approved text exists: create `terms.html` from an existing page (same header and footer), add it to
`sitemap.xml`, and link it where the HTML comment in every footer says "Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
links go here".

## Facts about the website (checked in the code)

- The website is informational. It describes seven service areas and has no online ordering, payments,
  accounts or log-in.
- Visitors can contact the company by phone, email, WhatsApp and the contact form (see the privacy draft).
- It offers a downloadable company profile (`Asknetic-Company-Profile-web.pdf`).
- It shows the logos of organisations described as clients (home and About Us pages). Written permission to
  show them is still an open owner item in `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.
- It links to third-party services: WhatsApp, Instagram and an embedded Google Maps frame.
- The footer reads "© <year> Asknetic Enterprises Ltd. All rights reserved."

## Topics for the owner and adviser to decide (no text drafted)
